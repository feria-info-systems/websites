Our Services

From outline health checks to specialized nursing. we bring professional care to your home so your family stays safe and comfortable.

Catheter & Patient Care

Our skilled nurses provide safe catheterization and attentive care for elderly or bedridden patients, ensuring comfort and dignity.

Medication & Health Education

Benefit from expert medication management and practical health education to support recovery and long-term wellness.

Ongoing Health Monitoring

We closely monitor vital signs and health status, adapting care plans to promote safety and optimal outcomes.

Blood Pressure & sugar monitoring

Regular checks for patients managing hypertension, diabetes, and other chronic conditions. Early detection saves lives.

Wound Care and Dressing

Professional cleaning and dressing of surgical wounds, diabetic wounds, pressure sores, and injuries  done right at home.

Day and Night Monitoring

Short shifts, long shifts, overnight  we're flexible. We fit into your family's schedule, not the other way around.

Health Education & lifestyle guidance

We teach patients and families how to manage conditions, eat well, and live healthier  knowledge is power.

Injection and Administration

IM, IV, and subcutaneous injections performed safely by our qualified nurses no need to visit a clinic.

Nurse Concierge


Our Nurse Concierge Service provides you with a dedicated, registered nurse who supports you throughout your hospital appointments helping you understand medical information, assisting with NHIS procedures, communicating with healthcare professionals, and ensuring you receive the guidance and care you deserve.

Our nurses are fully licensed and certified by the Nursing and Midwifery Council of Ghana, giving you trusted professional support every step of the way.

 Physical Presence 

Unlike virtual health services or phone consultations, your nurse physically accompanies you to every appointment in the room, by your side, advocating in real time.

Patients Advocacy 

 Someone who speaks up for you. Your nurse doesn’t just observe she asks the hard questions, challenges unclear advice, and makes sure your voice is heard, even when you don’t know what to say. 

Diaspora- Friendly 

We bridge the gap for Ghanaians abroad. Real-time updates sent to your family in the UK, US, Canada so they’re never left waiting and wondering.  

NHIS Expertise 

NHIS paperwork and processes can be confusing and time-consuming. We handle it so you don’t lose time, money, or access to the care you’re entitled to. 

Medication Clarity 

We make sure you fully understand your prescriptions what each medication does, how to take it, and what to watch out for before you ever leave the facility.  

Aways Available 

Health doesn’t wait for business hours, and neither do we. We’re available around the clock, every day of the week, with a quick response guarantee.  

Plain Language

We translate complex medical language into simple, everyday words so you understand your own health and can make confident, informed decisions.

(Pre/Post) Natal care

We provide Nurse Escort Services for antenatal visits, helping mothers receive proper support, guidance, and monitoring during pregnancy and after delivery. We also support postnatal recover

Home-Base Care

Beyond appointments, we bring healthcare to your home because comfort, privacy, and convenience matter just as much as the treatment itself.

Secure Records

We keep detailed records of every appointment, diagnosis, and medication so you always have a reliable health history when you need it most, whether for a future visit

Certified Professionals

All our nurses are registered with the Nursing and Midwifery Council of Ghana trained, certified, and held to the highest professional standards. You’re in qualified hands, always.
