Our Services

At Pro-Home Health LTD Care, we pride ourselves on delivering exceptional, compassionate care. Each service is designed with your comfort, safety, and wellbeing in mind. Our approach is rooted in clinical excellence and genuine concern for every patient we serve.

Our services are tailored to each individual, ensuring you or your loved one receives personalized attention and support. We believe every patient deserves care that respects their unique needs and circumstances.


Catheterization 

Safe catheter insertion and ongoing management to prevent infections, with careful hygiene monitoring. 

Elderly & Bedridden care 

Helping our elders with bathing, feeding, mobility  with dignity, love, and patience. Your parents deserve the best.

Post- Surgery care

Professional wound monitoring, vital sign checks, and medication supervision after surgery for safe, fast recovery.

Medication Administration

Making sure your loved one takes the right drugs at the right time no errors, no missed doses.

Blood pressure & Sugar monitoring

Regular checks for patients managing hypertension, diabetes, and other chronic conditions. Early detection saves lives.

Wound care & Dressing

Professional cleaning and dressing of surgical wounds, diabetic wounds, pressure sores, and injuries  done right at home.

Day & Night Nursing

Short shifts, long shifts, overnight, we're flexible. We fit into your family's schedule, not the other way around.

Health Education & Lifestyle Guidance

We teach patients and families how to manage conditions, eat well, and live healthier  knowledge is power.

Injection & Administration

IM, IV, and subcutaneous injections performed safely by our qualified nurses no need to visit a clinic.

IV Fluid Administration

Intravenous fluid therapy at home, professionally supervised. keeping patients hydrated and on track.

Nurse Concierge


Our Nurse Concierge Service provides you with a dedicated, registered nurse to support you during hospital appointments by explaining medical information, assisting with NHIS processes, and ensuring you receive the guidance and care you need.

All nurses are fully licensed by the Nursing and Midwifery Council of Ghana.
